The Classical Method

Our Mat Pilates certification teaches Joseph Pilates' original repertoire, with an emphasis on the mind-muscle connection and motor control.

1. The Fundamental Principles

Breathing, concentration, control, centering, precision, and flow. Learn why Pilates is much more than "doing ab work."

2. Classical Repertoire (Mat)

Anatomy and biomechanics of the 34 original exercises. Learn general exercise modifications and when to refer participants to a healthcare provider.

3. Class Programming

How to structure a 45–60 minute class that flows smoothly and keeps participants engaged.
