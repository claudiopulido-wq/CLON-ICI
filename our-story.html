OUR STORY

ICI is an online fitness training company based in Texas. We build practical courses for people who want to learn how to plan and lead safe, effective training sessions.

How ICI started

ICI was created to make practical fitness education available online. Many people who want to lead training sessions need clear guidance they can follow at their own pace, from anywhere. We built our courses with that in mind: short lessons, real-world examples and a clear path from the first lesson to the final assessment.

Our mission

Our mission is to help people learn how to plan and lead safe, effective training sessions through clear, practical online courses.

Our team

ICI is a small team focused on online education and fitness training. Before a course is published, its content is reviewed by a certified fitness trainer.

How our courses work

Our courses are fully online and self-paced. Each course combines short video lessons with a final assessment. Students who pass receive a certificate of completion from ICI. Our courses are educational: they do not replace advice from a physician, and a certificate of completion is not a professional license.

The technology behind our courses

ICI's online training platform is built on technology developed by GES Académico, a Certification and Evaluation Entity (ECE-515-22) accredited by Mexico's CONOCER. ICI's courses award a certificate of completion; they are not CONOCER certifications.
