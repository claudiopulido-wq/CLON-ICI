Online Course

Online course · Intermediate

Train With a Plan: Intermediate Strength & Conditioning

Go from working out to training with a plan: know how hard to push, how to progress week by week, how to move well under load and how to recover.

  • 24 video lessons
  • Self-paced, on any device
  • Certificate of Completion
Watch a free lesson Enroll now

Who this course is for

  • You already exercise regularly and know the basic movements.
  • You want a clear, structured plan instead of random workouts.
  • You want to learn how to progress safely and recover well.

Not for: Complete beginners, or anyone with an injury or medical condition who has not been cleared by a health professional.

What you'll learn

  • Measure your effort with RPE and reps in reserve (RIR).
  • Apply progressive overload and simple periodization.
  • Squat, hinge, push, pull and carry with good technique under load.
  • Train power and basic plyometrics, and use heart-rate zones and intervals.
  • Recover better: mobility, sleep, fueling and reading fatigue.
  • Know the stop signs, train safely in the Texas heat and build your own 4–6 week block.

What's included

  • 24 video lessons in 7 modules · about 24 min in total
  • English subtitles and on-screen coaching cards
  • Learn at your own pace, on your phone or computer
  • Final assessment: 20 questions, 80% to pass, unlimited attempts
  • Certificate of Completion (PDF)
  • Option to request an in-person class with a coach

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?

Yes, this is an intermediate course. It is for people who already exercise and know the basic movements.

Can I watch on my phone?

Yes. The course works on your phone, tablet or computer. Sign in with the same Google account and you continue where you left off.

Is this a professional certification?

No. You get a Certificate of Completion that shows you finished an ICI online course. It is not a professional license, a government certification or an accredited credential.

What if I don't pass the final assessment?

You need 80% or more to pass, and you can try again as many times as you need. Review the lessons and retake it whenever you're ready.

Can I train with a coach in person?

Yes, you can request an in-person class from your portal. Our team contacts you to agree on a day, time and place.

Is this medical advice?

No. The course is educational. Talk to a health professional before starting any exercise program, and stop if something hurts.
